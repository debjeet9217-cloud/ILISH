FROM RIVER TO TABLE

How does Bengali food reach the table? A continuous journey through river, harvest, and tradition.

01
Where it begins

THE RIVER

Bengal's rivers have shaped the region's food traditions for generations, bringing fresh fish to everyday tables.

02

THE FRESH CATCH

Hilsa, Rui and Katla are carefully selected according to season, freshness and traditional Bengali cooking.

03

Paturi is wrapped in banana leaf and gently steamed, allowing the fish, mustard and spices to cook together.
